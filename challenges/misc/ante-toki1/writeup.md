# ante toki nanpa wan

## Write-up

This challenge is written in toki pona, a minimalist conlang with ~120
words and very little grammar.

The hole track describes computer science concepts in that language.

This first challenge starts with an additionnal description that includes:

> utala ale li sama ni: utala li pana sona e ijo pi ilo sona. o toki e
> nimi ona kepeken toki Inli. toki sina ni o kepeken nimi wan taso.
>
> ...
>
> o kepeken nasin ni lon toki sina ale: o pana e toki sina kepeken
> nimi sama ni: len{toki sina}

which means

> All challenges are similar to this: the challenge teaches a thing
> related to computers. Tell its name using English. This speech of
> yours should use only one word.
>
> ...
>
> Use the following method for all that you say: give your speech
> using words similar to this: `len{your speech}`.

---

The easiest way to solve this challenge is to learn the conlang, or at
least the basics, which is possible in a short amount of time :-)

There are short video tutorials and short tutorials that cover the
basics pretty quickly.

Other options include:

- Word by word translation with an online tool or a custom script with
  a dictionnary + looking up the basics of the grammar
- Using this ML translator
  https://huggingface.co/spaces/Jayyydyyy/english-tokipona-translator
  and figuring the weird parts with the method mentioned above
